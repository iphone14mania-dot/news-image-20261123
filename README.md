A newspaper front page that sums up Monday 23 November 2026 in one 1024×1024 image: 35 stories across 6 continents. The three leads are the COP31 climate talks collapsing in Antalya after the EU refused a deal with no mention of fossil fuels, a Russian drone collapsing a five-storey block in Dymer with 14 people missing, and suspected Ukrainian sea drones sinking the Russian corvette Merkury off Benghazi. Around them: a key-numbers strip, a region-counts bar, ten feature cards (from the Cikande factory fire to Verstappen in Las Vegas, Liverpool 4–0 Manchester United, Alcaraz's first ATP Finals title and India closing in on day five in Wellington), a numbered world map, 22 more one-line briefs, and a "Date Check" box naming 21–22 November stories deliberately left out. Every claim is attributed to its source; nothing is presented as fact without attribution.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` to `out/entry.png`. No network, no randomness — both runs write identical bytes.
